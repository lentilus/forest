    A species is a function from the type of finite sets to the type of sets . Using the axiom of univalence we show that splits into connected components, indexed by the natural numbers such that the -th connected component is precisely the classifying type of the symmetric group in letters . In that spirit any species is given by a family of group actions , allowing us to think about species using Univalent Group Theory
