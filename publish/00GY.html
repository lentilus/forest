    Definition. Type of Groups [00H0]

      The type of groups is the unary sum (i.e a wrapped copy) of the type of pointed connected 1-types .

      It comes with an equivalences and its inverse . A group is term in the type of groups .

      Idea. [00HJ]

        In univalent mathematics there is an interesting way to think about groups. Every pointed connected 1-type carries a group structure in natural way: The composition of paths in the loop space is associative and every path is reversible. In fact every group arises as the loop space of some pointed connected 1-type. We will use results from the Symmetry Book that explores this idea in great detail.

        We give all the definitions necessary to state the Orbit Stabilizer Theorem, which will help us prove the Class Formula, an important tool for computing the groupoid cardinality of 1-types, which is key for decategorifying a lot of our constructions.
